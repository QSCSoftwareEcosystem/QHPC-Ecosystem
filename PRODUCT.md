# EQO-QSC Product Brief

## Product

EQO-QSC is the Ecosystem for Quantum Orchestration: a Django-based web workspace for composing, running, and inspecting quantum–HPC workflows across the Quantum Science Center software ecosystem.

## Audience and job

The primary audience is QSC scientific software developers and researchers working with quantum applications, error correction, orchestration, and leadership-class computing. The interface should help them understand what is available, launch a workflow quickly, see execution state, inspect provenance, and consult ChatQEC without losing their operational context.

## Product character

EQO-QSC should feel like a credible scientific control plane: modern, technically exact, calm under dense information, and visibly connected to quantum computing. It should not look like a generic SaaS analytics dashboard or a marketing landing page.

## Confirmed constraints

- Preserve the Django application and existing API behavior; visual work must not replace or bypass the backend.
- Use the QSC identity: Quark Red `#AA1E2E`, Force Blue `#131E29`, Wave Gray `#CFD2D3`, carbon black, the supplied QSC mark, and Open Sans.
- Keep the high-resolution static Canvas2D binary field as atmosphere and the compact QEC ASCII sequence as an explanatory instrument.
- ChatQEC must remain available as a full workspace and also be reachable contextually.
- Maintain keyboard access, explicit state labels, responsive behavior, reduced-motion behavior, and legible scientific data.
- Never invent worker availability, run results, benchmarks, or scientific claims; render live application data and honest empty states.

## Primary proof

The first viewport should show the product doing its job: a researcher can recognize the active orchestration environment, choose a published workflow, begin composition, see recent execution state, and understand service readiness without crossing a wall of summary cards.
